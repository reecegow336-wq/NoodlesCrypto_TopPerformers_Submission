\- Data-quality and validation checks

\- Power BI dashboards and interactive reporting

\- DAX measures, ranking, time intelligence and Dynamic Top N

\- Technical documentation and stakeholder reporting



The final Power BI solution includes five reporting pages:



1\. Executive Overview

2\. Platform Analysis

3\. Time Series Analysis

4\. Currency Deep Dive

5\. Executive Storytelling
